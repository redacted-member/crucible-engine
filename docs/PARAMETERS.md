## Pro EQ (`fx.eq_pro`)

Sixteen-band precision parametric equaliser: bell, shelves, tilt, band-pass, notch, all-pass and low / high cuts up to 96 dB per octave (Butterworth, Bessel, Linkwitz-Riley or resonant), every curve matched to its analog prototype up to 20 kHz so that nothing cramps near Nyquist. Each band works on stereo, left, right, mid or side and can be dynamic (gain follows the key level above or below a threshold; internal or external key). Minimum, natural or linear phase (the latency is reported); analytic response curve and output spectrum as views; automatic output gain.

## Transient Shaper (`fx.transient`)

Level-independent transient shaper. A fast and a slow envelope follower read the louder channel; their ratio in dB is positive on an onset and negative in the decay. ## Exciter (`fx.exciter`)

## Shaper (`fx.shaper`)

Waveshaper with a user-drawn transfer curve: 2 to 64 points (x and y within -1..1, x ascending) with a curvature per segment (y = y0 + (y1 - y0) * t^(4^curve), 0 is straight), optionally symmetric (the positive half is drawn and mirrored). The curve is set with crucible_set_shaper_curve, published to the audio thread as an immutable table, cross-faded in over 10 ms and saved in patches; the "curve" view returns it for drawing. Drive (pre gain), output, a latency-aligned mix, an optional DC block and 1x to 8x oversampling in the half-band chains. The default curve is the identity: with 0 dB drive the module passes the input through bit for bit (delayed by the reported latency). A curve that ends short of +-1 or below it clips there. Latency is the oversampling filters, reported.

## Multiband Drive (`fx.multiband_drive`)

Three-band distortion. Linkwitz-Riley crossovers (24 dB per octave, minimum phase, band sum flat) split the input into low, mid and high bands; each band runs through its own anti-aliased saturation model (the ten of fx.saturator, second-order antiderivative curves inside a linear-phase half-band oversampler: 2x in Draft, 4x in Standard and 8x in High, the high band one stage higher) with its own drive (0 to 36 dB), output, mix, solo and bypass. The bands are summed, mixed against the delayed input and trimmed by the output. A band at 0 dB drive or bypassed is not shaped at all: with every band so, the sum is the crossover's all-pass sum, flat within 0.1 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz and phase-coherent with it. Drives, outputs, mixes, solo and bypass glide (two 10 ms one-poles) and the crossover frequencies move over about 50 ms; a model change takes the band's wet share to zero, switches and brings it back. The wavefolder's drive range is a third of the control, the tape model's two thirds. Latency is the oversampling filters, reported; the bands and the dry path share it.

## Cabinet (`fx.cabinet`)
