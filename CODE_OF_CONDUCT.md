# Code of Conduct

Crucible Engine is a technical project that works best when people can disagree about code without it getting
personal. These rules apply in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any space that represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Argue about designs, measurements and code, not about people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear before concluding that it is wrong.
- Give credit. Say where an idea came from, and follow the clean-room rule in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- Accept review. Maintainers may ask for changes; contributors may disagree with reasons.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, threats, doxxing, sexual attention nobody asked for.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly re-opening settled decisions without new evidence.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a
problem, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting (see `SECURITY.md`) or contact a maintainer directly
rather than a public thread. Reports are handled in confidence. A maintainer who is involved in a report does not
take part in deciding it.
